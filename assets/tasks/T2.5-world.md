# T2.5 — World maps

> Read [`_SHARED.md`](_SHARED.md) first. Implementer produces the assets; a separate Reviewer accepts them by inspection (human-in-the-loop).

Redefined during review (2026-09-30): the first cut shipped the raw per-region sector grids and
terra tables as JSON (`assets/world/`), which is faithful to the disk layout but not reviewable and
not what a port wants. The 1987 layout exists because of 512K of RAM; we unpack it.

## Dependencies
T0.1, T2.2 (per-region atlases, for the previews), T2.2.1 (master atlas — the `master.png` layer
indexes it; conversely the master atlas takes its "used tiles" from these maps, so the two tools
run in order: `extract_master_atlas.py`, then `extract_maps.py`).

## Inputs (read-only)
- ADF `src/assets/image` (sector pools, region maps, terra blocks) via
  `tools/decode_map_data.load_regions`
- `src/fmain.c`: `file_index` (615-626), `doorlist` (233-326), door landing rules (1892-1955),
  `xfer` (2625-2645), the quicksand drop (1784-1789); `fsubs.asm` `px_to_im` (542-617) and `prox`
  (1590-1611) for walkability

## What is extracted
`tools/extract_maps.py` → `assets/maps/` (format: [`assets/maps/README.md`](../maps/README.md)):
1. **Overworld** — regions 0-7 stitched into one 2048×1024-tile map (`xreg`/`yreg`,
   `fmain.c:2983-2987`). The tileset region of a cell follows from its position.
2. **Interior spaces** — regions 8/9 are one shared 1024×256-tile sheet with two tilesets. From
   every entry (door landing point, stargate, quicksand drop) flood-fill the 8×8 sub-tiles the hero
   may stand on (`px_to_im` + `prox`: types 1 and ≥10 block; 8/9, 12 (Shard) and 15 (openable door)
   do not). Entries that reach the same tiles are one space → **62 building interiors** (region 8),
   **5 dungeons** (tombs, dragon cave, maze caves, spider pit, troll cave; region 9) and the
   **astral plane** (region 8, via the doom-tower stargate). Each is cropped to its reachable tiles +
   neighbouring walls + 1-tile margin; cells belonging to another space are blanked (tile 0).
   Deviation from source (user decision): the three mis-wired cabin-yard gates (PROBLEMS.md P27)
   are corrected to their own cabins; originals are kept in `map.json` as `fix.source_yc2`.
3. Per map two **index layers** — `tiles.png` (8-bit: original tile id) and `master.png` (16-bit:
   master-atlas index, which carries collision) — plus `map.json` (origin, tileset, entries with
   citations) and a `preview.png` rendered from the real tiles with entries ringed in magenta.
4. `tools/decode_map_layers.py` reads the layers back (importable `load_map`, CLI `--cell`/`--dump`).

Walkability lives in the tileset, not the map: the master atlas key was extended with
`feature_type` + `subtile_mask` (T2.2.1) so a master id alone gives art, shadow mask and collision.

## Outputs
- `assets/maps/{overworld,astral_plane,dungeons/*,interiors/*}/{map.json,tiles.png,master.png,preview.png}`
- `assets/maps/maps.json` (index), `assets/maps/README.md` (format), `assets/maps/verify.json`
- `tools/extract_maps.py`, `tools/decode_map_layers.py`, `tools/tests/test_extract_maps.py` (aid)

## Implementer self-check (optional aid)
- Every used (region, tile) pair resolves to a master tile with identical pixels and collision; no
  map cell lacks a master id; reachable areas of different spaces never overlap; the overworld
  layer equals the eight region tile grids; `load_map` round-trips every shipped map.

## Manual verification (human, by perception)
Work through T2.5's page in the review app (items from `assets/maps/verify.json`); **look at the previews**:
1. Overworld: the continent you know, no seams at region borders, door rings on the buildings.
2. Dungeons: five separate cave systems, each complete, entrances where you remember them.
3. Interiors: one building per map, its door(s) ringed, nothing from a neighbour inside the box.
4. Sanity: the app's counts (69 maps, 62 interiors, 5 dungeons, layers present) agree.
Report ACCEPT/REJECT with the maps viewed.
